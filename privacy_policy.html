Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Inspired Model School and Girl College Larkana ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application Inspired Model School (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide our school management services:

  • Student Information: Name, roll number, class, father's name, and phone number
  • Academic Data: Attendance records, fee payment status, exam results, and assignments
  • Login Credentials: Username and password for portal access
  • Device Information: Basic device information required to run the app

2. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely for:

  • Managing student records and academic performance
  • Tracking fee payments and attendance
  • Communicating important school announcements
  • Providing access to study materials and assignments
  • Improving our educational services

3. Data Storage and Security

All data is stored securely using Supabase cloud database with industry-standard encryption. We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Data is only accessible to:

  • Authorized school staff (teachers, administrators)
  • The student and their parents/guardians
  • School management for administrative purposes

5. Children's Privacy

Our App is designed for use by students of Inspired Model School and Girl College Larkana. We collect student information only with the knowledge and consent of parents or guardians. Parents may contact us to review, update, or delete their child's information.

6. Internet Access

The App requires an internet connection to access the student portal. We do not collect any browsing data or track your internet activity beyond what is necessary to provide our school management services.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal information stored in our system
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to school record requirements)
  • Contact us with any privacy concerns

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
